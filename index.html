Math drills — “to the T”

The step-by-step method, exactly as taught. Every human is diploid, so people × 2 = alleles. Learn the 3 moves and the numbers can change all they want.

Move 1
major = 1 − MAF
Major frequency from minor.
Move 2
frequency = count ÷ (people × 2)
Count → frequency.
Move 3
count = frequency × (people × 2)
Frequency → expected count.
